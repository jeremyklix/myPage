Jeremy Klix for Abbotsford City Council

Building a modern, vibrant and inclusive city for everyone.

The Mission

To bring proactive, forward-thinking solutions to Abbotsford that address long-term infrastructure needs and build a resilient, inclusive city where everyone can live work and play.

About Me

I have called Abbotsford my home for over 15 years. I was drawn to the community through my time I spent working and pursuing education here. I attended the University of the Fraser Valley where I earned a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Information Systems and a Diploma in Architectural Drafting. Following university, I have worked in Abbotsford as a software developer helping businesses use technology to identify amd solve their problems.

Outside of my professional work, I have continued to enjoy investing my time in the people and community around me. I have served as a youth mentor through Archway Community Services, and am the moderator on my church's leadership team. I'm also an avid rock climber and love to get outside and spend time at locals crags.

Campaign Priorities

Modern Transport

Reduce congestion by deploying smart intersections and expanding bus services, and improve options for active transport.

Quiet Streets

Install traffic microphones to uphold noise bylaws and reduce noise pollution.

Urban Forests & Parks

Expand our urban forests, increase the tree cover in our urban centres, and add additional parks with new development.

Vibrant Culture & Gathering Spaces

Provide residents with places to create, gather, play and socialize. Upgrade our sports fields, and recreation and community centres.
